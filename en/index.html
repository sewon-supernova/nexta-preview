How it works

Here's what happens when you get in touch.
There's no commitment until you've reviewed and agreed to the terms.

Talk to us
  1. 1

    Tell us about yourself

    Send us an email with a little about what you create, what you'd like to try, or any questions you have.

  2. 2

    Talk through the details

    We'll discuss your plans, the support available, and the terms, including any fees, compensation, and contract length.

  3. 3

    Make a plan to go live

    Once you've reviewed and agreed to the terms, we'll choose a topic, set a date, and start preparing for your first stream.
