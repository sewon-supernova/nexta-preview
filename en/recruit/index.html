Q&A

FAQs

QI've never done a LIVE. Is that OK?

Absolutely. We start with you from what to talk about and how to prepare.

QAre there really no quotas?

Yes. No required hours or number of LIVEs. Keep a pace that fits your life.

QDoes the agency take a cut of my earnings?

No. 100% of what you receive from your LIVEs (the creator share) is yours. NEXTA takes nothing from it.

QHow do I get the ¥100,000 bonus?

It is paid when you reach the set goal within 3 months of joining. We explain the goal at your interview.

QCan I do this alongside work or school?

Yes. We plan your schedule around the time you have. Please check any side-job rules at your workplace yourself.

QWhat if I don't speak Korean?

No problem. You can start by streaming in Japanese. When you try a Korean project, we check what's needed together.

QI'm with another agency right now.

Please check your current contract. TikTok's rules may stop you from switching right away. Tell us your situation and we'll explain.

QWhat if I want to leave?

Contracts run for 1 year and renew automatically. If you want to leave, let us know at least 6 months ahead.

QDoes applying or interviewing cost anything?

No. You never pay registration or lesson fees to join.
