Privacy Policy

Adopted September 13, 2026

Operator Information Privacy Policy Website Terms of Use

The NEXTA Formation Team (the "Operator") handles information received through inquiries made via this site in accordance with the following policy.

1. Information we receive

When you contact us by email, we receive your email address, name, current creator activity, any social media accounts you choose to include, and the contents of your inquiry.

Selecting "Contact us by email" opens your email app. Your selected activity status and inquiry are not sent automatically from this page.

You do not need to include identity documents, bank account details, passwords or similar information in your first inquiry.

2. How we use information

We use the information to answer your inquiry, understand the activities and support you are interested in, and contact you about that inquiry. If we use it for another purpose, we will provide any explanations and obtain any consent required by law.

3. Management and sharing with third parties

We limit the handling of information to what is needed to respond to inquiries and take the necessary measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure and similar risks.

We do not provide personal information to third parties without your consent, except where provided by law. If we entrust information handling to external services, such as email storage providers, we will carry out the necessary management and oversight within the stated purposes of use.

4. Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry or required by law, and properly delete information we no longer need.

5. Site access information

This site does not use its own analytics tools or advertising tags. The Operator does not set cookies to track visitors.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for details on how it handles information.

GitHub Privacy Statement

6. Requests to access, correct or stop using information

To request access to, correction or deletion of your information, or to ask us to stop using it, please contact us using the details below. We will verify your identity and the details of your request, and respond in accordance with applicable law.

7. Policy updates

We will review this policy when our information handling changes and publish the updates on this page. Where changes require consent or other procedures by law, we will follow those procedures.
